# Originals: pop-culture gag scenes

Short scenes of Clawd parodying games, music and movies, made for this fork.
They are AI-drawn with the toolkit in `kit/` (Clawd's body and eyes come cell
for cell from the official frames; costumes, props and effects are layers on
top), and follow the rules in `docs/originals-style.md`. Each scene is one
authoring script; run it with `node <folder>/<scene>.js` to rebuild its GIF,
previews (`<folder>/preview/`) and catalog entry.

## Movies (movies2 set)

Made for this fork; AI-drawn with the originals kit. Caricature only: no
likenesses, logos or quoted lines. Shared helpers for these scenes live in
`movies/props_m2.js` (bookends, curly / floppy / shaggy / messy hair,
eyebrows, grin, laser sword, clash sparks, gold chain, sweat drop, gull).
